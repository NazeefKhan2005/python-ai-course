# Hybrid work policy (2024)

> This policy is archived. It was replaced on 2 March 2026 by "Working hours and hybrid work" (hr-002).

## Working hours

* Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Friday.
* Outside core hours, work when it suits you and your team.

## Office days

* Work from the office **at least 1 day a week**, on a day agreed with your team.

## Working from home

* The company pays a one-off **home-office allowance of 200**, claimed with receipts.

## Working from another country

* You may work from another country for **up to 10 working days a year**, with your manager's approval.

Write to people@colibri.example.
