# Bogotá office notes

A page anyone in the Bogotá office can edit. Please keep it short and friendly.

## Getting in

* The office is on the 4th and 5th floors. Reception opens at 07:30 and closes at 19:30.
* Visitors sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge.
* Forgot your badge? Reception gives you a day pass.

## Kitchen

* The kitchen rota is on the fridge door. Each team cleans for one week.
* Label your food with your name and the date. The fridge is emptied every Friday at 16:00.
* The good coffee machine is on the 5th floor.

## Getting here

* Bike parking is in the basement, with 30 places and a shower on the 4th floor.
* There is no car parking for employees. Taxis home after 22:00 are paid by the company (see Expenses and business travel).
