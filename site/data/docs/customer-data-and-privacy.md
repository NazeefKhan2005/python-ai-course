# Customer data and privacy

Customers, couriers and restaurants trust us with their data. Use only the data you need, for the purpose it was collected for, and keep it only as long as this page allows.

## Data classes

| Class | Examples | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Menus, published prices, press releases | Can be shared with anyone |
| Internal | Team documents, dashboards without personal data | Employees only |
| Confidential | Order history linked to a customer ID, restaurant contracts, salaries | Only people who need it for their work |
| Restricted | Payment details, exact home addresses, courier location traces, identity documents, health information | Named people only; never in spreadsheets, chat or AI tools |

When you are not sure, treat data as the higher class.

## How long we keep data

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Orders and invoices | 5 years (tax law) |
| Customer profiles | 24 months after the customer's last order, then deleted or anonymised |
| Support chats and emails | 12 months |
| Courier location traces | 90 days |
| Office CCTV recordings | 30 days |

## Data for analysis

* Faro gives analysts **pseudonymised** data: customer names, phone numbers and emails are removed, and customer IDs cannot be linked back to a person without a separate key that only the Privacy team holds.
* Never try to identify a person in pseudonymised data.
* Never export Restricted data to spreadsheets or personal folders.
* Access to Confidential and Restricted data is reviewed every quarter.

## Requests from customers

Customers can ask to see, correct or delete their data.

1. Forward the request to privacy@colibri.example **within 1 working day**.
2. The Privacy team checks the person's identity and answers **within 15 working days**.

## Data incidents

If you think personal data was lost, stolen or sent to the wrong person, report it to security@colibri.example **within 1 hour**. The Data Protection Officer decides whether to notify the authorities, which must happen within 72 hours of the company learning about the incident.
