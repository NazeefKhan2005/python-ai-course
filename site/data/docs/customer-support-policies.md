# Customer support policies

## Response times

| Channel | First response |
|---|---|
| In-app chat | within 2 minutes |
| Email | within 24 hours |
| Safety reports | immediately, by phone |

## Late deliveries

Compare the delivery time with the promised time the customer saw at checkout.

| How late | What the customer gets |
|---|---|
| Less than 20 minutes | An apology; no credit |
| **20 to 44 minutes** | **A credit of 20% of the order subtotal** |
| **45 minutes or more** | **The delivery fee refunded, plus a credit of 20% of the subtotal** |

* Credits are added to the customer's account and **expire after 90 days**.
* A customer can receive at most 3 late-delivery credits in 30 days. Team leads can approve more.

## Problems with the food

* **Missing item:** refund the item's price.
* **Wrong order:** offer a full refund or a new delivery, as the customer prefers.
* **Food quality** (cold, spilled, damaged): a credit of up to 50% of the subtotal; ask for a photo when possible.
* **Allergies or food safety:** refund the order in full and report it to the restaurant partnerships team the same day.

## Cancellations

* Customers can cancel for free until the restaurant accepts the order.
* After that, cancellation is free only if the order is already 30 minutes later than promised.

## Safety

If a customer, courier or restaurant worker reports a threat, an injury or an accident, stop and call the operations lead on duty at once. Never ask the person to wait in the chat queue.

## Tone

Be honest about delays and do not promise times the courier cannot meet. Use the customer's name and keep messages short.
