# Working with restaurant partners

## Commission

| Order type | Commission on the subtotal |
|---|---|
| Delivery by a Colibrí courier | **18%** |
| Pick-up by the customer | **9%** |
| Delivery, during a new partner's first 60 days | 12% |

The customer pays the delivery fee; it does not go to the restaurant.

## Payouts

* Restaurants are paid **every Tuesday** for the orders of the previous week (Monday to Sunday).
* Each payout comes with a statement listing every order, its commission and any refunds charged to the restaurant.
* Refunds for problems caused by the restaurant (missing or wrong items) are charged to the restaurant. Refunds for late deliveries are paid by Colibrí.

## Joining Colibrí

1. The restaurant signs the partner agreement.
2. It sends its menu with photos, a food-safety certificate and its bank details.
3. Partnerships checks the documents and sets up the menu.
4. The restaurant goes live, usually **within 10 working days** of signing.

## Quality

* Target **preparation time: 15 minutes** from accepting an order.
* Restaurants whose average rating stays **below 3.8 for 4 weeks** get a review call with their partnership manager and a plan to improve.
* Repeated food-safety complaints can lead to a pause while we investigate.

## Contact

Fatima Diallo, Partnerships Lead, and the partnerships team handle all restaurant questions.
