# Expenses and business travel

## Claiming expenses

1. Pay with your company card if you have one, or with your own money.
2. Submit the claim in **Ledger within 30 days** of the expense. Claims older than 30 days need approval from the Finance Manager.
3. Attach a **receipt for every expense above 20**. For smaller amounts, a short description is enough.
4. Your manager approves the claim in Ledger.
5. Claims approved by the 20th of a month are paid with that month's salary. Later claims are paid the month after.

We do not reimburse alcohol, fines (parking or speeding), personal entertainment or upgrades you choose for comfort.

## Travel limits

| Expense | Limit |
|---|---|
| Meals in your own country | up to 35 a day |
| Meals abroad | up to 55 a day |
| Hotel in Bogotá, Lima or Quito | up to 120 a night |
| Hotel in other cities | up to 100 a night |
| Your own car | 0.30 per km |
| Team events | up to 30 per person per quarter |

* **Flights:** economy class. Book through the travel desk (travel@colibri.example) at least 14 days before the trip when you can.
* **Taxis and ride-hailing:** reimbursed for work trips. Between 22:00 and 06:00, take a taxi instead of public transport; we always reimburse it.
* **Trains and buses:** standard class.

## Before you travel

* Your manager approves every trip in Ledger before you book.
* Trips abroad also need the travel desk's approval, at least 10 working days before you leave.
* Keep your laptop with you, use the VPN, and follow the IT security policy.
