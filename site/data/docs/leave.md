# Leave and time off

Request all leave in Nido. Your manager approves it, usually within 2 working days.

## Annual leave

* Full-time employees get **25 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, plus the public holidays of the country where their office is. Part-time employees get a proportional number of days.
* In your first and last year, you get a share of the 25 days for the months you work.
* For more than 3 days in a row, ask at least 2 weeks before.
* You may **carry over up to 5 unused days** to the next year. Carried-over days expire on **31 March**. Other unused days are lost.

## Sick leave

* Tell your manager as early as you can on the first day.
* You can **self-certify up to 3 days** in a row. From the fourth day, upload a doctor's note to Nido.
* Sick leave is paid in full for up to 30 days a year; after that, local law applies.
* If you get sick during annual leave, those days become sick leave when you send a doctor's note.

## Parental leave

| Leave | Length | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| Birth parent | 20 weeks | full pay |
| Other parent | 8 weeks | full pay |
| Adoption (main carer) | 20 weeks | full pay |
| Adoption (other parent) | 8 weeks | full pay |

Parental leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected birth or adoption date. The other parent can take the 8 weeks in up to 2 blocks within the child's first year. Tell the People Team at least 8 weeks before your leave starts.

## Other paid leave

* **Bereavement:** 5 days for a partner, child, parent or sibling; 2 days for other close relatives.
* **Volunteering:** 2 days a year for work with a registered charity.
* **Moving house:** 1 day a year.
* **Birthday:** a day off in the week of your birthday.

## Unpaid leave

You can ask for up to 3 months of unpaid leave after 1 year with Colibrí. The People Team decides each request with your manager.
