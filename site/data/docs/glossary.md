# Glossary

**Anchor days**: the days a whole team works from the office (see Working hours and hybrid work).

**AOV (average order value)**: the total of delivered orders divided by their number.

**Change freeze**: a period with no deploys except incident fixes, from 26 November to 2 December.

**Commission**: the share of an order's subtotal that a restaurant pays to Colibrí.

**Courier**: an independent partner who delivers orders. Couriers are not employees.

**Dark kitchen**: a kitchen that cooks only for delivery and has no dining room.

**Data class**: Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted (see Customer data and privacy).

**Faro**: the data platform, with dashboards and SQL access.

**Feature flag**: a switch that turns a feature on or off without a deploy.

**Focus Friday**: no internal meetings after 13:00 on Fridays.

**Incident commander**: the person who coordinates the response to a SEV1 incident.

**Late order**: a delivered order that took longer than the promised time.

**Ledger**: the expenses tool.

**Nido**: the HR portal for leave, payslips and personal details.

**Payout**: the weekly payment to a restaurant, made every Tuesday.

**Peak hours**: 12:00 to 14:00 and 19:00 to 21:00 local time; no deploys then.

**Pick-up order**: an order the customer collects from the restaurant.

**Postmortem**: a blameless written review of an incident, due within 5 working days.

**Promised time**: the delivery time shown to the customer at checkout.

**Pseudonymised data**: data with names and contact details removed, whose IDs cannot be linked to a person without a separate key.

**Repeat order**: an order from a customer who had ordered before.

**SEV1, SEV2, SEV3**: incident severity levels (see On-call and incident response).

**Subtotal**: the price of the food in an order, before the delivery fee and discounts.

**30-day repeat rate**: the share of new customers who order again within 30 days of their first order (see Data, metrics and experiments).
