# Welcome to Colibrí

Colibrí is a food-delivery company. Customers order from local restaurants in our app or on the web, and independent couriers bring the food to their door. Colibrí was founded in Bogotá in 2024 and opened to customers on 1 January 2025 in five cities: Bogotá, Lima, Medellín, Cali and Quito.

About 220 people work for Colibrí. Couriers are independent partners, not employees; the courier pages in this handbook explain how we work with them.

## Our values

* **Customers first, honestly.** Tell customers the truth about delivery times, even when the news is bad.
* **Fair to partners.** Restaurants and couriers succeed with us or we do not succeed.
* **Small steps, measured.** We test changes and keep the ones that work.
* **Safety before speed.** No delivery is worth an accident.

## How to use this handbook

Each page starts with a short header: who owns it, when it was last updated and its status. Pages with the status **current** are in force. Pages with the status **archived** are kept for reference only; when an archived page and a current page disagree, the current page applies. Amounts in this handbook are plain numbers in our reporting currency.

If something is unclear or out of date, write to the owner of the page.
