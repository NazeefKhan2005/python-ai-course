# Benefits

Benefits start on your first day, unless this page says otherwise.

## Health

* **Private health insurance** for you, your partner and your children. Colibrí pays 80% of the cost; the rest is taken from your salary.
* **Counselling:** a confidential support line with up to 6 free sessions a year with a counsellor. The People Team never learns who uses it.
* **Wellbeing allowance:** 40 a month for a gym, a sports club, yoga classes or a wellbeing app, claimed in Ledger.

## Food

* On office days, order lunch through the Colibrí app with your **staff lunch credit: up to 15 per office day**. Unused credit does not carry over to the next day.
* Fruit, coffee and tea are free in every office.

## Equipment

* A laptop, a headset and a second screen for the office. Laptops are replaced every 3 years.
* The home-office allowance is described in "Working hours and hybrid work".

## Money

* Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month.
* **Referral bonus:** 1,000 when someone you referred passes their probation period of 3 months.

## Time off

Annual leave, parental leave and other paid leave, including your birthday off, are described in "Leave and time off".
