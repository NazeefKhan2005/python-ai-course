# Courier safety

Couriers are independent partners who choose when to work. Their safety comes before delivery times.

## Insurance

Colibrí pays for **accident insurance** that covers couriers from the moment they accept an order until they deliver it or it is cancelled.

## Equipment

* A helmet is required on bicycles, scooters and motorbikes. Couriers who are seen without one are paused for 24 hours.
* A reflective vest or jacket is required **after 18:00**.
* An insulated delivery bag is given free to every new courier and replaced free once a year.

## Working time

* The app stops offering orders after **10 hours online in one day**. Couriers can go online again after a break of 8 hours.
* Couriers can decline any order. Declining never lowers their pay rate.

## Weather

* In **heavy rain**, the app reduces the delivery radius, and couriers can decline orders without any effect on their account.
* When the authorities issue a **red weather alert** for a city, Colibrí pauses deliveries there until the alert ends.

## Incidents

* The **SOS button** in the courier app calls the emergency number and alerts our operations team.
* The operations team calls the courier back **within 5 minutes**.
* Customer support never asks a courier to continue a delivery after an accident.

## Behaviour

Customers, restaurants and couriers must treat each other with respect. Couriers can report abuse in the app; we investigate every report within 2 working days.
