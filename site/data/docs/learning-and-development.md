# Learning and development

We want everyone at Colibrí to keep learning. This page explains the time and money you can use.

## Learning budget

* Each employee has a **learning budget of 1,200 a year** for courses, books, conferences and exam fees.
* Spending up to 500 at a time needs no approval: claim it in Ledger with the receipt.
* Spending more than 500 at a time needs your manager's approval first.
* The budget runs from 1 January to 31 December. Unused budget does not carry over.
* Travel to a conference is paid from your team's travel budget, not from your learning budget.

## Learning days

* You have **5 learning days a year** for courses, conferences or self-study.
* Book them in Nido as "learning day", like annual leave.

## Learning inside Colibrí

* **Data Academy:** a talk every first Thursday of the month, 16:00 to 17:00, given by the Data Team or a guest. Recordings are on the intranet.
* **Mentoring:** after 6 months with us, you can ask the People Team for a mentor from another team. Mentors and mentees meet for an hour every 2 weeks for 6 months.
* **Language classes:** free English, Spanish and Portuguese classes twice a week, online, in small groups.

## Sharing what you learned

After a course or conference paid from your budget, write a short summary for your team or give a 10-minute talk at a team meeting.
