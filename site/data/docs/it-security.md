# IT security

These rules protect our customers, partners and colleagues. Breaking them can lead to disciplinary action.

## Accounts and passwords

* Use the company password manager for every work password.
* Passwords must have **at least 14 characters**. Never reuse a work password anywhere else.
* **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is required for every company account.
* Engineers with production access and all administrators use a hardware security key.
* Never approve an MFA request you did not start. Report it to Security at once.

## Devices

* Your laptop has full-disk encryption and locks automatically **after 5 minutes** without use. Lock it yourself whenever you walk away.
* Install updates within 7 days, and critical security updates within 48 hours.
* USB storage devices are blocked. Share files through the company drive.
* Do not store customer data on personal devices.
* On public Wi-Fi, always use the VPN.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone **within 1 hour**: write to security@colibri.example or post in the #security channel. Security can lock and wipe the device remotely. Reporting quickly matters more than knowing exactly what happened.

## Phishing

* Be suspicious of messages that are urgent, ask for passwords or payments, or contain unexpected links or attachments.
* Use the "Report phishing" button in your email. Do not forward the suspicious message to colleagues.
* We run phishing simulations every quarter. Clicking a simulated link is not punished; it means you get a short training.

## Access

* You get the access your role needs, nothing more. Managers review their team's access every quarter.
* When you change teams, access you no longer need is removed.
