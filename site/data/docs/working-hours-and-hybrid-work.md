# Working hours and hybrid work

This policy replaces the 2024 hybrid work policy from 2 March 2026.

## Working hours

* A full-time contract is 40 hours a week.
* **Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00** local time, Monday to Friday. Be available for meetings and messages during core hours.
* Outside core hours you choose when to work, between 07:00 and 20:00. Agree regular patterns with your manager.
* **Focus Fridays:** no internal meetings after 13:00 on Fridays.

Customer support, operations and on-call engineers work in shifts. Their shift rotas replace core hours.

## Office days

* Work from your office **at least 2 days a week**.
* Each team chooses its **anchor days**, the days the whole team is in the office. If a team has not chosen, the anchor days are Tuesday and Thursday.
* New employees work from the office every day during their first 2 weeks.

## Working from home

* You need a quiet place to work, a stable internet connection and a way to take calls privately.
* Never work with Restricted data in public places (see Customer data and privacy).
* **Home-office allowance:** a one-off 300 for a chair, a desk or a screen, claimed in Ledger with receipts, plus 25 a month towards your internet bill, paid with your salary.

## Working from another country

* You may work from another country for **up to 20 working days a year**.
* Request it in Nido at least 10 working days before you travel. Your manager and the People Team approve it.
* Keep core hours in the time zone of your home office.
* Use the VPN at all times, and do not take Restricted data with you.
* Days abroad do not carry over to the next year.
